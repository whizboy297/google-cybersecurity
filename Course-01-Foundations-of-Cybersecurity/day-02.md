## Key Concepts

- A computer virus is malicious code that attaches to legitimate programs and spreads when executed.
- Malware is a broad term that includes viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, spyware, and other malicious software.
- The Morris Worm demonstrated how quickly malware could spread across networks and led to the creation of Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs).
- The Equifax breach highlighted the importance of protecting Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and keeping systems patched.
- Social engineering attacks exploit human behavior rather than technical vulnerabilities.
- The digital age has increased both the opportunities and risks associated with technology.
- The eight security domains organize cybersecurity into major areas of knowledge and responsibility.
- Different cybersecurity tasks belong to different security domains depending on their objectives.

## My Notes

- Organizations must protect customer information throughout its entire lifecycle, including secure disposal.
- The eight CISSP security domains provide a framework for understanding the different areas of cybersecurity.
- Security Operations is one of the most common domains for entry-level security analysts.
