## My Notes

- Security frameworks provide organizations with a structured approach to protecting systems and data.
- The primary goals of security frameworks are protecting PII, managing organizational risks, identifying weaknesses, and aligning security with business goals.
- Security controls are safeguards that reduce risks and can be technical, administrative, or physical.
- The CIA Triad is the foundation of cybersecurity:
  - Confidentiality ensures only authorized users can access information.
  - Integrity ensures data remains accurate and unchanged.
  - Availability ensures systems and information are accessible when needed.
- The NIST Cybersecurity Framework is a voluntary framework that helps organizations identify, manage, and reduce cybersecurity risks.
- Cybersecurity professionals are expected to act ethically by protecting confidential information, respecting privacy, following company policies, and complying with laws.
- Privacy protection focuses on safeguarding personal information from unauthorized use.
- Insider threats are dangerous because employees often have legitimate access to sensitive systems and information.
- Organizations should strengthen their defenses rather than retaliate against attackers.
