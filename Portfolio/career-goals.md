# Career Goals

## Short-Term Goal

Obtain an entry-level Cybersecurity Analyst or SOC Analyst role after completing the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and my MSc in Cyber Security.

## Medium-Term Goal

Earn industry certifications such as CompTIA Security+ and gain experience in incident response, threat detection, and vulnerability management.

## Long-Term Goal

Become a Cybersecurity Engineer or Security Architect who designs secure systems and helps organizations defend against cyber threats.
